02 — The path

Cut from soccer.
Kicked anyway.

Marjan grew up in Raleigh playing club soccer at a high level and expected to keep going. He was cut from his high school team — by his own account, over a mix-up about what position he played. He joined the football team the following week.

What followed was not a straight line. Three schools, three years of waiting behind other kickers, and then two seasons that made him one of the more accurate kickers in college football.

2021–2023 · East Carolina

Three years, no attempts

Redshirted as a freshman, then appeared in 17 games as the Pirates' kickoff specialist. Never attempted a field goal. Entered the transfer portal looking for a job to compete for.

2024 · South Alabama

16 of 17

Won the starting job and missed once all year. Named second-team All-Sun Belt in his first season as a placekicker — and immediately became one of the most sought-after kickers in the portal.

2025 · Kansas

The Big 12 test

Beat out the field for the Jayhawks' starting job, went 14 of 17 with a career-long 55, and did not miss a single extra point in 40 tries. Third-team All-Big 12 and a Lou Groza Award semifinalist.

2026 · Pittsburgh

First NFL camp

Signed with the Steelers as an undrafted free agent and competed through training camp behind an established veteran. Handled all kicking duties in the second preseason game. Waived on August 23.

Now
