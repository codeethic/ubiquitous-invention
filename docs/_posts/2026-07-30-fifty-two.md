date: 2026-07-30

52 feels... weirdly like a victory lap you didn't expect to survive long enough to take.

Not because everything's awesome. It isn't. The knees bitch. The recovery takes longer. You make old-man noises standing up. The push-up goal is literally tied to your age now—52 on the birthday because why the fuck not.

But there's also this quiet realization that you've outlived a lot of people you loved. Family. Friends. Coworkers. Folks who should still be here. Every birthday starts to feel less like "I'm getting old" and more like "I got another one." That changes the math.

Work? Meh. You're counting the days more than climbing ladders. Money isn't about buying dumb shit anymore. It's about freedom. About looking at retirement and thinking, "Maybe I can just... stop." These are themes running through a lot of my conversations.

The Tamara and the boys matter more than anything. As long as Jacob and Jackson outlive me, I'm good. Everything else is just details.

And there's the bittersweet part. Marriage is hard. Politics make you want to throw the phone into the ocean. The world feels meaner than it used to. So 52 isn't some Hallmark-card "aging gracefully" bullshit. It's more like:

> "I've got enough scars to know what matters now."

Still gonna lift.

Still gonna yell at the Canes.

Still gonna cry over the boys.

Still gonna tell my family and friend I love them.

Still gonna say "Fuckers." at the news.

Still gonna chase one more good trip, one more bourbon, one more hockey season.

Welp.

Let's fuckin' go.
