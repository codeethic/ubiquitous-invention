Fuck, Bruv.

The honest answer is: **I have no fucking advice.**

I'm not a kicker. I've never stood 40 yards away from a goalpost with thousands of people waiting to see whether I can do the one thing I've spent a significant portion of my life training to do.

But I *do* know something about being a guy who gives a shit about the outcome.

That miss is nothing.

Not "nothing" because it doesn't matter. It obviously matters to 34. It's the fucking NFL, and every rep is an audition.

But it tells him almost nothing about whether he belongs there. It tells the world nothing about the man he is. 

He got this far. He spent weeks traveling around with 15 kickers and was the **only one who got signed**. He knows what's in front of him, and he has plans for different outcomes. And that outcome will be "even sweeter."

So maybe the wisdom is just:

**Don't turn one kick into a story about yourself.**

You missed a kick.

Then you get another kick.

Then another.

And eventually somebody decides whether you're good enough.

That's it.

And honestly, Bruv, if I were standing next to him I'd probably say:

> "34, I got nothin'. I'm just a 50-year-old software guy who has never kicked a football in his fucking life. But I know this: don't let one miss make you smaller. You're there because you earned your ass into that building. Go kick the next fucking ball."

And then I'd hug the man and cry.

Because that's what I do. Here's some advice for the author: give your balls a tug, old man.
