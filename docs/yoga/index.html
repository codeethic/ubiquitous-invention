What we teach

Three things, taught well.

We do not run twenty formats. We run vinyasa, we run flow, and we sit. That is the whole studio.

Vinyasa

The core of the studio. One breath, one movement, built into a sequence with a real peak and a real landing. Warm, continuous, and honest about being work.

60 – 90 minAll levels

Slow Flow

Same vinyasa logic, half the tempo. Longer holds and time to actually arrive in a shape before leaving it. The one to take when your week has been loud.

60 – 75 minAll levels

Meditation

Guided sitting, breathwork, and silence — as its own class, not five minutes bolted onto the end of a flow. No experience needed and nothing to believe in.

30 – 60 minEveryone

Who is teaching

Maya Ashley

Founder · vinyasa and meditation · teaching since 2011

Vinyasa is where I actually live as a teacher. I care about the count — where the breath lands, how a sequence builds toward one shape and then unwinds from it — and I will not run a class where the transitions are an afterthought.

The meditation came later and changed how I teach the flow. Moving well is a way of getting quiet enough to sit; sitting is what makes the moving mean anything. Most weeks I teach both, and I would rather you did both too.

  • E-RYT 500, Yoga Alliance
  • Vinyasa Krama training, 2014
  • Trauma-informed teaching certificate, 2019
